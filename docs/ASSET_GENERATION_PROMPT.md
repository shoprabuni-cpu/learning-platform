# Visual asset generation prompt

Copy this prompt into ChatGPT when preparing the visual asset pack.

---

Act as art director and asset-production partner for “Learning Platform,” a Ghana-focused learning product for students, teachers, parents, and schools.

Create a cohesive, professional visual system that feels art-directed by a skilled human design team, not like generic AI output or stock ed-tech branding.

BRAND
- Use Ghana-inspired red, gold, and green as restrained accents. Balance with warm off-white and deep charcoal; maintain readable contrast.
- Feel welcoming, capable, bright, and calm—not childish or corporate.
- When people appear, use contemporary Ghanaian study settings, varied appearances, natural light, and believable poses. Avoid stereotypes, invented cultural symbols, inaccurate uniforms, or fake school logos.
- Prefer considered editorial photography direction or clean hand-crafted illustration. Keep lighting, texture, line weight, and composition consistent.
- Avoid purple-blue gradients, glowing orbs, glassmorphism, random floating shapes, plastic-looking faces, excessive 3D, clutter, and decoration without purpose.
- Do not include words, interface text, watermarks, fake logos, or small illegible writing. Leave useful negative space for product text.
- Never combine separate deliverables into a collage. Generate each asset individually.

ASSETS
1. Landing hero banner with separate desktop and mobile compositions.
2. Lesson, study, and classroom editorial images.
3. Subject icons for Mathematics, English, Science, Social Studies, and ICT.
4. Dashboard empty states for no lessons, no quiz activity, and no upcoming exams.
5. Consistent achievement badge illustrations.
6. Short, subtle motion concepts for quiz feedback, lesson progress, loading, achievement unlock, and celebration.
7. Optional effects for correct answer, incorrect answer, completion, and badge unlock.

DELIVERY
- First provide inventory, art direction, palette, filenames, and placements.
- Produce each asset individually. Do not blindly crop desktop banners for mobile.
- Use transparent backgrounds for icons and badges where useful. Include concise alt text.
- Keep motion subtle, respect reduced-motion settings, and never rely on motion to communicate essential information.
- Sounds must be optional, off by default, short, quiet, and user-triggered. No autoplay, background music, or startling effects.
- If you cannot generate animation or sound directly, provide an implementation-ready motion recipe or sound brief with duration and file format.
- Label generated assets as concepts requiring human review for cultural accuracy, accessibility, and consistency.
